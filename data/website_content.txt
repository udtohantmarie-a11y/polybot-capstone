1. About Talibon Polytechnic College
Talibon Polytechnic College (TPC) is located in San Isidro, Talibon, Bohol. It describes itself as a student-centered institution committed to quality education, research, and community development in Talibon, Bohol.
1.1 Vision
An institution committed to provide quality educational opportunities and academic programs that empower students to become competitive and responsive to the needs of the community.
1.2 Mission
To provide quality educational opportunities and support services, strengthen innovative research and extension, and produce competent individuals in technological and professional fields for community transformation and development.

2. Academic Programs
Talibon Polytechnic College currently lists seven (7) academic programs on its website. Each is a 4-year baccalaureate program. As of this compilation, the individual program pages on the website do not yet contain expanded curriculum details (course lists, units, or specializations) beyond the summaries below — each individual program page displays a "No details yet — more details about this program will be available soon" notice.
2.1 Bachelor of Arts in English Language (BAEL)
A 4-year program that focuses on the study of English language, literature, and culture. It equips students with strong language skills, critical thinking, and analytical abilities.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-arts-in-english-language
2.2 Bachelor of Arts in Political Science (BAPS)
A 4-year program that explores government systems, politics, and international relations. It develops critical thinking, research, and analytical skills.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-arts-in-political-science
2.3 Bachelor in Early Childhood Education (BECED)
A 4-year program that prepares students to teach and care for young children (0-8 years old). It focuses on child development, curriculum design, and teaching strategies.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-in-early-childhood-education
2.4 Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (BSA)
A 4-year program that focuses on crop production, animal science, and agricultural management. It develops skills in farming, agribusiness, and sustainable agriculture.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-science-in-agriculture
2.5 Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems (BSAIS)
A 4-year program that combines accounting principles with information systems and technology. It focuses on designing, implementing, and managing accounting systems, ensuring accurate financial data management and compliance.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-science-in-accounting-information-systems
2.6 Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSC)
A 4-year program that focuses on crime prevention, investigation, and correction. It develops skills in law enforcement, forensic science, and criminal justice.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-science-in-criminology
2.7 Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS)
A 4-year program focusing on designing and implementing computer-based information systems and ICT solutions to meet business needs. It equips students with skills in systems development, business process analysis, and ICT implementation. Graduates can work as Systems Analysts, IT Project Managers, or Information Security Specialists.
Program page: https://www.tpc.edu.ph/academics/bachelor-of-science-in-information-systems

4. Student & Institutional Services
4.1 Guidance and Counseling Center (GCC)
The Guidance and Counseling Center (GCC) is a unit under the Office of Student Affairs and Services Division (OSAS) that provides programs and activities to support the holistic development of students. It aims to help students cope with academic, personal, social, and career concerns through counseling, testing, information dissemination, consultation, and referral services.
Guidance and Counseling Services
I. Individual Inventory Service
The systematic collection, evaluation, and interpretation of comprehensive information about students.
II. Information Service
The systematic collection and distribution of information through various methods and programs to support students in their personal, social, academic, emotional, occupational, and spiritual development.
III. Counseling Service
Gives students a chance to understand themselves better, grow as individuals, and solve problems through one-on-one or group counseling.
IV. Testing Service
Psychological testing used as a tool for assessment to describe an individual's abilities, interests, aptitudes, preferences, and characteristics.
V. Follow-Up Service
Assesses the effectiveness of interventions, monitors student progress, and provides additional assistance to promote academic success, emotional well-being, and personal development.
VI. Prevention and Wellness Service
Supports TPC bona fide students in their personal efforts to maintain good health and achieve social balance.
VII. Career Development Service
Guides students in identifying their interests, developing career goals, and preparing for their professional journey.
VIII. Placement Service
Assists students in making informed decisions about their academic journey.
IX. Referral Service
Connects counselees with agencies, organizations, or individuals that can offer more effective support to address their issues.
X. Consultation Service
Highlights the importance of seeking the opinions and decisions of others to positively impact a program.
Other Services — Student Peer Support Program
The TPC Wellness Hub is the official name of the Peer Facilitators' Circle of Talibon Polytechnic College. Members are TPC students who have completed the required workshops and training to become certified Peer Facilitators. They are selected and officially recognized by the Guidance and Counseling Center, meeting qualifications set by the Student Development Office. The group takes part in student activities and programs organized by the GCC, and helps in counseling sessions and student lectures under the supervision of Guidance Counselors and Staff.

4.2 Registrar's Office
Vision
The Office of the Registrar, guided by its mandate, aspires to be a department of professional excellence fostering students' success by assisting them in achieving their academic goals with recognition from a student-centered environment.
Mission
Provide excellent academic support services with emphasis on the college mission by modelling the highest standards of the registrar profession in providing exemplary services to students, faculty, staff and other stakeholders while ensuring the utmost academic records integrity.
Goals
1.	Perform the mandated functions of the college through an effective and efficient records management system.
2.	Maintain, update, safeguard and preserve students' academic records.
3.	Provide accurate records/documents to clients and other stakeholders.
Core Values (T.I.P.S.)
•	T — Teamwork
•	I — Integrity
•	P — Professionalism
•	S — Service
Tagline
"From enrolment to employment: Guiding your Academic Ascent"
Services Offered
•	Course Registration/Enrolment — registering for subjects each semester, including adding, dropping, and changing of subjects.
•	Record Keeping — maintaining comprehensive student records, including transcripts, grades, and personal information.
•	Transfer Credit Evaluation — evaluating and processing transfer credits from other institutions to determine their equivalency.
•	Changing/Adding/Dropping of Subjects — processing student requests to change, add, or drop subjects.
•	Form 137 Requests to Senior High School — requesting the permanent record of a student's SHS academic performance.
•	Enrollment Verification — providing verification of a student's enrollment status for various purposes.
•	ID Validation — ensuring the authenticity and accuracy of identification documents during the semester.
•	Leave of Absence — processing student requests for temporary leaves of absence from academic studies.
•	Degree Verification — verifying degrees earned by current or former students to third parties such as employers.
•	Release and Processing of Transcripts / Certifications — releasing official transcripts or certifications for academic, employment, and other purposes.
•	Graduation Application — processing applications for graduation and required paperwork.
•	Academic Calendar — providing information on registration periods, holidays, and exam schedules.
•	Graduation Clearance — reviewing academic records to confirm all graduation requirements have been met.
•	Student Evaluation/Advising — guidance on academic progress, course selection, and degree requirements.
•	Release of Diploma — issuing diplomas to graduates and to students who lost or damaged their original diploma.
•	Grade Processing — overseeing the submission and processing of grades by faculty each semester.
•	HEMIS Submission — submitting Higher Education Management Information System data to CHED Regional Office 7 every academic year.
•	Compliance and Reporting — ensuring adherence to legal standards and preparing reports for accreditation and government agencies.
•	Academic Policy Implementation — enforcing academic policies on grading, academic standing, and appeals.
